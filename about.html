Who builds OWL

Legal AI should not just answer. It should verify.

A verification layer on existing research

OWL is built for attorneys who already research—and who need to know whether a citation will survive scrutiny.

OWL AI Agency is founded by Hobie Cunningham and is based in Ingleside, Texas. The product is a citation verification layer that sits on top of work you already do: briefs, memos, and research generated by people or by other tools.

We do not replace Westlaw, Lexis, or a lawyer. OWL checks whether cited authorities can be confirmed against public legal sources so a human reviewer can decide what to file.

  • Accountability for citations, not a substitute for legal judgment
  • Built to run beside existing research workflows
  • Human review of every output is required

Generative tools can produce fluent answers with invented or misapplied citations. OWL’s job is the opposite: show the verification trail so the risk is visible before a filing goes out.

Run the public Carpenter v. United States example on the verify page, then talk with us about per-document or firm verification.
